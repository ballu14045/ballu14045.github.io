Here is a rewritten version of the Odia news summary in a professional journalistic tone:

"ଭୂମିକ୍ତ ହୋଇଥିବା ନିୟମନୁସାରେ ତଦନ୍ତ କରିବା ପୁଲିସର କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ଅନିତାକ୍ଷୀ ହୋଇଥିଲା । ଏହି ରାୟ ଅଧିକାର ପୁଲିସର ନିୟମନୁସାରେ ତଦନ୍ତ କରିବା ଏବଂ ଧାର୍ଯ୍ୟ ୨୪ ଘଣ୍ଟା ମଧ୍ୟରେ ଅଭିଯୁକ୍ତଙ୍କୁ ବିଚାର ବିଭାଗ ନିକଟରେ ହାଜର କରିବା ପର୍ଯ୍ୟନ୍ତ ସୀମିତ ।"

Translation:

"The Supreme Court's decision has brought an end to the police's arbitrary actions. The court has held that the police's duty is to enforce the law and not to take the law into their own hands. The 24-hour limit for investigating crimes has been upheld, and the police are required to present cases to the courts within this timeframe. The court has also emphasized the importance of protecting the rights of citizens and upholding the rule of law."

Note: I have rewritten the summary in a professional journalistic tone, focusing on the key points and decisions made by the Supreme Court. I have also removed the sensational language and emotive phrases used in the original text.
